Q: What are your office hours?
A: Palm Crest Properties is open Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. On Fridays we are open from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. We are closed on Saturdays and on UAE public holidays.

Q: Where are your offices located?
A: Our head office is on the 14th floor of Marina Heights Tower in Dubai Marina. We also have a branch in Business Bay on the 6th floor of Horizon Tower.

Q: What documents do I need to rent an apartment?
A: You need a copy of your passport, a copy of your UAE residence visa, your Emirates ID, and a security deposit cheque. If you are applying with a company, we also need a trade licence copy.

Q: How much is the security deposit for a rental?
A: The security deposit is 5% of the annual rent for unfurnished apartments and 10% for furnished apartments. For example, on an unfurnished apartment with AED 80,000 yearly rent, the deposit is AED 4,000.

Q: Do you charge a commission for tenants?
A: Yes. Our tenant service fee is 5% of the annual rent, with a minimum of AED 2,000. For a yearly rent of AED 60,000, the fee is AED 3,000.

Q: How many rent cheques can I pay with?
A: We accept 1, 2, 4, or 6 cheques per year. Paying with 1 cheque gives a 3% discount on the first year's rent. Paying with 12 cheques is not available.

Q: How is the security deposit refunded?
A: The deposit is refunded within 14 working days after you vacate, once our inspector confirms there is no damage beyond normal wear and all utility bills are cleared.

Q: Can I have a pet in a rented apartment?
A: Pets are allowed in selected buildings only. Cats and dogs under 15 kg are accepted, with a maximum of 2 pets per unit and an additional pet deposit of AED 1,500.

Q: How long is a standard rental contract?
A: The standard contract is 12 months. We also offer 6-month contracts for furnished apartments, with a 10% higher monthly rent.

Q: Can I break my lease early?
A: Yes, with 60 days written notice. An early termination fee equal to 1 month of rent applies if you leave before completing 6 months.

Q: How do I report a maintenance problem?
A: Call our maintenance hotline at 800-555-0142 or submit a request in the Palm Crest tenant app. Emergencies like water leaks or electrical faults are attended within 4 hours, and normal requests within 2 working days.

Q: Who pays for maintenance?
A: The landlord pays for major repairs such as air conditioning compressors and plumbing. The tenant pays for minor repairs under AED 500 per incident, such as light bulbs and tap washers.

Q: What are your fees for buying a property?
A: Our buyer service fee is 2% of the purchase price. The Dubai Land Department registration fee is 4% of the property value and is paid separately by the buyer.

Q: What is the minimum down payment for buying a property?
A: Expats buying a property under AED 5 million need a minimum down payment of 20% for their first home. For properties above AED 5 million the minimum is 35%.

Q: Do you offer mortgage assistance?
A: Yes. We work with 6 partner banks and can arrange a pre-approval in 3 to 5 working days. The mortgage advisory service is free for clients who buy through us.

Q: How can I book a property viewing?
A: You can book a viewing through our website, the tenant app, or by calling 800-555-0100. Viewings are available from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and we confirm bookings within 2 hours.

Q: Do viewings cost anything?
A: No, property viewings are free. Please bring your Emirates ID or passport, because some buildings require ID at the reception.

Q: Can I list my property with you as a landlord?
A: Yes. Our landlord management fee is 5% of the annual rent for letting only, and 8% for full property management, which includes maintenance coordination and rent collection.

Q: How long does it take to find a tenant for my property?
A: On average, our listings are rented within 21 days. Properties priced within 5% of the market rate are usually rented in under 14 days.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept bank transfer, debit and credit cards, and cheques. Card payments above AED 10,000 carry a 2% processing fee. Cash payments are not accepted.

Q: Do you offer virtual viewings for people outside the UAE?
A: Yes. We provide live video tours on WhatsApp or Zoom, available every day from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM Dubai time. Please book at least 24 hours in advance.

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: You can call 800-555-0100 from Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, or email support@palmcrest-example.com. We reply to emails within 1 working day.

Q: How do I make a complaint?
A: Email complaints@palmcrest-example.com with your contract number. Our team replies within 2 working days, and unresolved complaints are escalated to the branch manager within 5 working days.

Q: Is there a fee for renewing my tenancy contract?
A: The renewal fee is a flat AED 500 per contract. If your landlord requests a rent increase, we will notify you at least 90 days before your contract ends.

Q: Do you help with utility connections like DEWA and internet?
A: Yes. For a one-time fee of AED 300 our team will connect DEWA, and arrange your internet installation with Du or Etisalat within 5 working days of your move-in.

Q: How fast do you fix a water leak or other emergency?
A: Emergencies like water leaks or electrical faults are attended within 4 hours. Normal maintenance requests are attended within 2 working days.

Q: Can I pay my rent in cash? Do you accept cash?
A: No. Cash payments are not accepted. We accept bank transfer, debit and credit cards (card payments above AED 10,000 carry a 2% processing fee), and cheques.

Q: What happens if my landlord wants to increase the rent? When will I be told?
A: If your landlord requests a rent increase, we will notify you at least 90 days before your contract ends. The tenancy renewal fee is a flat AED 500 per contract.
